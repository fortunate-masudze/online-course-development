# Articulate Rise 360 – Sample Online Course

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates practical skills in online course development and instructional design using Articulate Rise 360.

**Course title:** Foundations of Digital Learning: Tools, Benefits, and Effective Practices

**Authoring tool:** Articulate Rise 360

**Project type:** Self-paced online learning course

## Course Development

The sample course was designed to introduce learners to digital learning, its benefits, characteristics and development over time.

The course includes:

- Course introduction and learning objectives.
- Structured learning content.
- Interactive flashcards introducing key digital learning terms.
- Accordion interactions explaining core characteristics.
- Interactive comparison activities.
- A timeline illustrating the evolution of educational technology.
- Multiple-choice knowledge checks.
- A concluding section.

## Instructional Design Approach

The course uses short, organised learning sections and interactive activities to support learner engagement and understanding.

Learning objectives guide the content, while knowledge checks provide opportunities for learners to assess their understanding.

## Technical Skills Demonstrated

- Articulate Rise 360 course authoring.
- Online content organisation.
- Interactive learning activity development.
- Assessment and knowledge-check creation.
- Course preview and quality assurance.
- SCORM 1.2 publishing for LMS deployment.
