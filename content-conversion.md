
# Content Conversion Process

## Introduction

I have experience transforming existing educational materials, including PDF documents and PowerPoint presentations, into structured self-paced online learning experiences.

My approach focuses on organising content, improving learner engagement, maintaining instructional clarity, and preparing materials for delivery through a Learning Management System (LMS).

## My Content Conversion Workflow

### 1. Review and Analyse Source Materials

- Review the supplied PDF documents, PowerPoint presentations, and other learning resources.
- Identify the learning objectives, key topics, and important concepts.
- Check the content for completeness, clarity, and consistency.
- Identify opportunities to improve the presentation of the material for online learning.

### 2. Organise the Learning Content

- Divide the content into logical topics and learning sections.
- Arrange information in a sequence that supports progressive learning.
- Develop clear headings, introductions, and topic summaries.
- Identify suitable supporting resources and learning activities.

### 3. Develop Digital Learning Content

I use digital authoring tools to transform learning materials into online formats.

Tools include:

- Articulate Rise 360
- iSpring Suite

Depending on the learning requirements, content may include:

- Interactive learning blocks
- Text and image-based explanations
- Presentations and downloadable resources
- Knowledge checks and quizzes
- Multimedia learning resources

### 4. Prepare Content for LMS Delivery

- Prepare digital learning materials for integration into Moodle.
- Use SCORM packages where appropriate.
- Organise course topics and learning resources.
- Ensure learners can easily navigate the course.

### 5. Review and Quality Assurance

Before delivery, I check:

- Content accuracy and consistency.
- Spelling, grammar, and formatting.
- Navigation and accessibility of learning resources.
- Functionality of interactive elements and assessments.
- Overall presentation and learner experience.

## Practical Experience

During my eLearning consultancy work with RUFORUM (October–December 2025), I converted PDF and PowerPoint learning materials into self-paced online Moodle courses using Articulate Rise 360 and iSpring Suite.

At Bindura University of Science Education, I also support Moodle course management, learning resource organisation, lecturer support, and the development of online learning materials.

## Outcome

The process helps transform traditional educational resources into organised, accessible, and learner-friendly digital courses suitable for online delivery.
