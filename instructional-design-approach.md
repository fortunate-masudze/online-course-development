
# Instructional Design Approach

## Introduction

My approach to instructional design focuses on creating structured, accessible, and engaging online learning experiences that support learners in achieving clearly defined learning outcomes.

I aim to ensure that digital courses are easy to navigate, logically organised, and appropriate for the intended audience.

## Key Principles

### 1. Learning Objectives

Each course or topic should have clearly defined learning objectives that explain what learners are expected to know or do after completing the learning activities.

### 2. Logical Content Structure

I organise course content into manageable sections and topics.

A typical online course structure may include:

- Course introduction and overview
- Learning objectives
- Topic-based learning materials
- Interactive activities
- Supporting resources
- Topic assessments or knowledge checks
- Final assessment

### 3. Learner Engagement

I consider different ways of presenting information to make online learning more engaging.

These may include:

- Interactive content
- Images and illustrations
- Videos and presentations
- Knowledge checks
- Quizzes and practical activities
- Downloadable learning resources

### 4. Clear Navigation and Signposting

Learners should understand where to begin, what to complete, and how to progress through a course.

I support this through:

- Consistent topic headings.
- Clear activity descriptions.
- Descriptive resource labels.
- Instructions for completing interactive content.
- Clearly identified assessments.

### 5. Assessment and Feedback

Assessments should align with the learning objectives.

I consider the use of quizzes, knowledge checks, assignments, and other assessment activities to help learners evaluate their understanding.

### 6. Accessibility and Usability

I aim to make course materials easy to access and use by considering:

- Readable text and consistent formatting.
- Clear instructions.
- Appropriate use of images and multimedia.
- Simple and predictable navigation.
- Compatibility with the intended LMS.

## Tools and Platforms

My instructional design and eLearning work has involved:

- Moodle LMS
- Articulate Rise 360
- iSpring Suite
- SCORM packages
- Google Workspace

## Practical Application

At Bindura University of Science Education, I support the organisation and management of Moodle courses and assist lecturers with preparing and presenting learning materials.

I have also contributed to the development of standard Moodle course layouts and navigation guidelines to promote consistency across online courses.

Through my RUFORUM consultancy, I gained practical experience converting existing educational materials into self-paced online learning experiences.

## Conclusion

My goal is to develop online courses that are well structured, learner-centred, easy to navigate, and suitable for effective digital delivery.
