
# Sample Online Course Structure

## Course Title: Introduction to Digital Learning

**Course Type:** Self-paced online course  
**Delivery Platform:** Moodle LMS  
**Target Audience:** University students  
**Format:** Online learning with interactive content and assessments

## Course Overview

This sample demonstrates how I would structure a Moodle course to provide learners with a clear and organised learning experience.

The course is divided into topics, with each topic containing an introduction, learning materials, supporting resources, and an assessment activity.

## Course Welcome Section

Welcome to Introduction to Digital Learning.

This course introduces learners to the use of digital technologies in teaching and learning.

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, learners should be able to:

- Explain the concept of digital learning.
- Identify common digital learning tools.
- Describe the benefits of online learning.
- Apply basic digital learning practices.

## Topic 1: Understanding Digital Learning

**Topic Introduction:**  
An introduction to digital learning, its meaning, development, and role in modern education.

**Learning Materials:**
- Interactive lesson
- Topic presentation
- Downloadable reading material
- Relevant video resource

**Learning Activity:**  
Reflect on how digital technologies are used in your learning environment.

**Knowledge Check:**  
A short quiz to assess understanding of the topic.

## Topic 2: Digital Learning Tools

**Topic Introduction:**  
An overview of tools and platforms used to support online teaching and learning.

**Learning Materials:**
- Interactive lesson
- Examples of digital learning tools
- Supporting reading materials

**Learning Activity:**  
Identify digital tools that can support teaching and learning.

**Knowledge Check:**  
A short quiz on digital learning tools.

## Topic 3: Effective Online Learning Practices

**Topic Introduction:**  
Exploring practices that support successful participation in online learning environments.

**Learning Materials:**
- Interactive lesson
- Learning guide
- Video resource

**Learning Activity:**  
Develop a personal plan for participating effectively in an online course.

**Knowledge Check:**  
A short topic assessment.

## Final Course Assessment

A final quiz covering all three topics.

## Course Navigation

The course should use consistent headings and clear activity labels.

Learners should be able to identify:

- What to read or watch.
- Which interactive lesson to complete.
- Which resources to download.
- Which activities to attempt.
- Which assessments to complete.

For SCORM activities, clear instructions should explain how to launch the content, navigate through it, and complete the activity.

## Assessment Structure

The following is an illustrative assessment structure:

| Assessment | Weighting |
|---|---:|
| Topic 1 quiz | 10% |
| Topic 2 quiz | 10% |
| Topic 3 quiz | 10% |
| Final course assessment | 70% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

## Note

This is a sample course design created to demonstrate my approach to online course structuring and instructional design. It is not a published or completed Moodle course.
